Structure now
-------------
The catalog is sourced from `/mock-items` via `lib/data/mockItemParser.ts`, seeded into PostgreSQL through Prisma, and accessed exclusively through `lib/server/products.ts`. Frontend data flows rely on `ProductListItem` (for lists/cards) and the simplified `Product` structure (for detail views), with price values stored and computed in cents to stay payment-ready. Filtering UI has been replaced by a single search field and a price sort toggle, while cart and favorites hydration is centralized in `components/layout/Providers.tsx`.

Fixes delivered
---------------
Mock data has been fully replaced by the ten curated items, filters/tags were removed across API and UI, and cart/favorites now persist correctly after refresh thanks to explicit store rehydration. The cart summary applies the requested shipping tiers, 5% bulk discount, and displays a localized VAT line. Prisma now targets PostgreSQL with a matching migration/seed pipeline, and `backend-guide.txt` documents setup, operations, and deployment considerations.
