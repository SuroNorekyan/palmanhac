Palmanhac Coffee Liqueur
Palmanhac Coffee Liqueur is a premium liqueur produced from orange and strawberry wines, blended with 100% Arabica coffee brewed using the Cold Brew method.
An intense, elegant, and contemporary drink.

Tasting Notes:
Deep aroma of fresh coffee and cocoa, with citrus and fruity nuances from the wine base. On the palate, it is full-bodied and velvety, with a long finish of coffee and subtle notes of cocoa.

Region: Setúbal Peninsula
Base: Orange and strawberry wine with 100% Arabica coffee (Cold Brew)
Type/Color: Coffee-colored liqueur
Alcohol Content: 21%
Bottle Size: 700 ml
Serving Temperature: 8–12 °C

Serving Suggestions:
Excellent as a digestive, served over ice or in coffee-based cocktails. Also perfect for desserts or pairing with dark chocolate.

Palmanhac Licor de Café
Palmanhac Licor de Café é um licor premium produzido a partir de vinhos de laranja e morango, combinados com café 100% Arábica preparado através da técnica Cold Brew.

Região: Península de Setúbal
Base: Vinho de laranja e morango com café 100% Arábica (Cold Brew)
Tipo/Cor: Licor de cor café
Teor Alcoólico: 21%
Formato: 700 ml
Temperatura de Serviço: 8–12 °C

Sugestões de Consumo:
Excelente como digestivo, servido com gelo ou em cocktails com café. Ideal também para sobremesas e combinações com chocolate amargo.
