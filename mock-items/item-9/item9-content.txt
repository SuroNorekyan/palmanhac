Palmanhac Spirit Cola 

Inspired by the refreshing taste of classic cola, Palmanhac Spirit Cola is an artisanal liqueur crafted from natural lemon wine, combining citrus notes with the intensity and slightly tangy character of cola.
A modern and vibrant fusion that captivates from the very first sip.

Tasting Notes:
Sweet and spiced aroma with hints of lemon, caramel, and natural cola. On the palate, it is smooth and refreshing, with a lightly toasted finish and a balanced touch of tangy cola.

Region: Setúbal Peninsula
Base: Natural lemon wine
Type/Color: Brown fruit liqueur (cola-colored)
Alcohol Content: 15%
Bottle Size: 700 ml
Serving Temperature: 8–12 °C

Serving Suggestions:
Ideal as a digestive, served over ice or in refreshing cocktails with citrus fruits and spices.
