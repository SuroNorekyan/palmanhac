Palmanhac 
Lemon Fortified Wine 
Price 8,77 €
Description:
Palmanhac Lemon Fortified Wine delights with an elegant, soft aroma highlighted by lingering lemon notes.
Its fresh, vibrant flavor of lemon peel is beautifully complemented by subtle hints of eucalyptus, ending with a bright and refreshing finish.

Tasting Notes:
Fresh and zesty aromas dominated by lemon, balanced with gentle eucalyptus nuances and a lively, refreshing aftertaste that brightens the palate.

Alcohol: 18% vol
Volume: 500 ml
Serving suggestion: Serve very chilled (8–10°C).
Perfect pairing: Ideal as a digestif, on the rocks, or in refreshing summer cocktails.

Awards:
