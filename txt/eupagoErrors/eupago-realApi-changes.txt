File: lib/payments/eupago.ts
- Added centralized request/response logging, live-environment warning, MB WAY status logging, and provider reference normalization helpers.
- Base URL normalization now only trims whitespace/trailing `/api` so EuPago requests keep whatever host the operator configured (e.g. `https://clientes.eupago.pt` or sandbox), avoiding marketing-site 404s.
- Header-mode authentication now sends `ApiKey`, `Authorization: ApiKey …`, and `X-Api-Key` simultaneously (body-mode keeps `apiKey` in the form payload). Card/MB WAY payloads stay minimal but snake_case, Multibanco posts the lean form (`id`, `valor`, `descricao`, `email`, `currency`).
