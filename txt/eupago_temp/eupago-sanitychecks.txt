Quick sanity checks (outside your app)

Run these two cURL calls. If these work, your app will too.

Credit Card (v1.02, header auth, form body)

curl -i -X POST 'https://sandbox.eupago.pt/api/v1.02/creditcard/create' \
  -H 'ApiKey: demo-74d6-183e-c5b9-480' \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/x-www-form-urlencoded' \
  --data 'id=test-order-123&value=66.49&currency=EUR&description=Test+Order&return_url=https://send-surgery-arrives-regular.trycloudflare.com/orders&customer_email=sandbox@example.com&customer_name=Demo&locale=en'


Expected: 200 with payment_url/paymentUrl and transaction_id/transactionId.

Multibanco (legacy body-auth)
