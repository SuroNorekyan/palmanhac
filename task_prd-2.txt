## 4) CART PAGE: SHIPPING, DISCOUNT & VAT (IVA) RULES
**Client rules (translated & implement):**
- Product naming: the “title” is the same string in both languages; localized descriptions differ.
- **Shipping conditions**:
  - If **order total >= 50 €** → **Delivery = free (0 €)**.
  - Else, by **bottle count** (sum of quantities):
    - **1–2 bottles:** delivery = **6 € total**
    - **3+ bottles:** delivery = **8 € total**
- **Bulk discount**:
  - If **10 bottles** (items count across cart) → **5% discount** on items subtotal (before shipping).
- **VAT/IVA display**:
  - The final total **includes** VAT (23%). Display a separate line:
    - EN: `"VAT included 11.22 €"` (format with dot decimal, 2 decimals)
    - PT: `"Inclui IVA 11,22 €"` (format with comma decimal, 2 decimals)
  - Compute VAT included: `vat = total - (total / 1.23)`
  - Round to 2 decimals (locale-appropriate formatting).

**Order of operations (implement clearly):**
1) `itemsSubtotal = Σ (price * qty)`
2) If total bottles ≥ 10 → `itemsSubtotal = itemsSubtotal * 0.95` (5% discount)
3) Compute **delivery**:
   - If `itemsSubtotal >= 50` → `delivery = 0`
   - Else:
     - If `bottles <= 2` → `delivery = 6`
     - Else `delivery = 8`
4) `total = itemsSubtotal + delivery`
5) `vat = total - (total / 1.23)` → format as above and display below total
6) Localize all labels (EN/PT). Use locale-specific decimal formatting:
   - EN: `11.22`
   - PT: `11,22`

**Acceptance checks:**
- Cart page displays discount line (if applicable), delivery fee according to conditions, final total, and a VAT/IVA line as per locale (exact labels above).
- Free shipping banner at the top remains consistent: “Free shipping from €50”.
